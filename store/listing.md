## Full description
Take command of a busy harbor in Ship Traffic Control Sim, a relaxing yet challenging ship traffic control game. Draw a route from each boat to its matching port, keep every vessel on course and prevent collisions at sea.

**How to play**
Drag your finger from a ship to the port with the same color and icon. Guide fishing boats to the fishing harbor, ferries to the passenger terminal, cargo ships to the container port and tankers to the fuel dock. Dock enough ships to clear the level.

**Features**
- 10 unique vessels: sailboats, speedboats, ferries, fishing trawlers, container ships, tugboats, cruise liners, oil tankers, patrol boats and submarines
- Unlimited levels with new islands, coastlines and ports in every stage
- Difficulty that grows as you play, with rush hours, sea fog and strong currents
- Calm Waters power to slow the sea when traffic gets busy
- Lifebuoys that save you from a collision
- Simple one-finger controls, easy to learn and hard to master
- Colorful 2.5D graphics and a calming original soundtrack
- Play offline, anytime
- Family-friendly for all ages, with no in-app purchases

If you enjoy boat games, port management, traffic control or casual puzzle simulators, Ship Traffic Control Sim is your next favorite harbor game.
