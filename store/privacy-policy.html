Privacy Policy

Ship Traffic Control Sim (package io.github.tilakpatel22.shiptrafficcontrolsim)
Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Ship Traffic Control Sim ("the game", "we") handles when you play it on Android.

Information the game stores

The game does not ask for, collect or transmit your name, email, contacts, location or any account information. Your progress and settings (current level, best level, ships docked, lifebuoys, music/sound/vibration preferences) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version and ad interactions. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change this anytime in Settings → Ad Privacy Options.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional.

Permissions

The game uses Internet and network-state access (to load ads) and vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
