Privacy Policy

Gamecept Studios
Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how Gamecept Studios ("we") handles information in our mobile games, including Ship Traffic Control Sim (the "Games"). Our Games are made for all ages, including children, and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and Google Play's Families Policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, logins, chat or social features. Game progress and settings (levels, ships docked, lifebuoys, sound and vibration preferences) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2. Advertising

Our Games are free and show ads from Google AdMob: short ads between levels and optional rewarded videos. Every ad request is marked as child-directed, under the age of consent, and limited to G-rated (family-friendly) content. This means:

  • No personalized or interest-based ads, and no remarketing.
  • The Android Advertising ID is not used; the permission is removed from our apps.
  • Google may process limited technical information (such as IP address, device type, OS version and ad interactions) only to show contextual ads, measure them and prevent fraud, as allowed for child-directed services.

Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps · AdMob and child-directed treatment

3. Parental controls

  • Links that leave the Game (Rate Us, Privacy Policy) are protected by a parental gate.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional and clearly labelled.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

4. Permissions

Internet and network access (to load ads) and vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings). Google's ads SDK may add technical permissions it needs to work. Our Games do not use the camera, microphone, contacts, files, location or advertising ID.

5. Data retention and deletion

We store no personal data on our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes all game data on the device. Parents may contact us at any time with questions or requests.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of personal data. As we hold no personal data, we will help direct any request to Google where relevant.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
